# 2026/27 Live Shadow Decision Evidence

## Outcome

The platform now has a season-long, immutable deadline evidence loop. It does
not change the production objective, rank branches differently, or execute any
FPL action. Its purpose is to replace repeated historical transfer-ranker
experiments with trustworthy evidence from the active 2026/27 rules, prices,
players, fixtures, and news regime.

## What is frozen before each deadline

- Official season, Gameweek, deadline, rules version, and rules payload hash.
- Data cutoff, bootstrap hash, fixture hash, and source-response hash.
- Squad, bank, free transfers, hit policy, and remaining/used chips.
- Production models, portfolio, planner version, and planner configuration.
- Every distinct legal root action generated by the production planner, not
  only the strongest branch for each chip.
- Transfers, hits, Wildcard/Free Hit squads, XI, ordered bench, captain, vice,
  expected points, horizon value, uncertainty, and opportunity cost.
- Available status/news, starting probability, and official set-piece-order
  fields for players in each branch.
- The selected action and raw deterministic ordering.

GW1 records every configured opening-policy profile. In-season records use the
connected team's real squad, selling prices, bank, free transfers, and chips.

## Post-Gameweek settlement

Settlement is blocked until the official bootstrap event is both `finished`
and `data_checked`. It then scores the exact frozen branches using official
event points and minutes, including:

- Legal autosubs and frozen bench order.
- Captain and vice-captain fallback.
- Triple Captain and Bench Boost.
- Transfer-hit deductions.
- Wildcard and Free Hit active squads.

The outcome preserves every pre-deadline counterfactual and reports selected
regret against only that frozen candidate set. It never reconstructs candidates
with information learned after the deadline.

## Operations

```powershell
# Before each deadline; team ID is required after GW1
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m fpl_intelligence.live_decision_evidence capture --team-id YOUR_TEAM_ID

# Coverage and finalization status
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m fpl_intelligence.live_decision_evidence status

# Only after official FPL scoring is data-checked
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m fpl_intelligence.live_decision_evidence settle --season 2026-27 --gameweek 1
```

The daily refresh accepts `-TeamId` and runs the capture automatically. The API
exposes read-only coverage at `GET /api/operations/shadow-evidence`.
